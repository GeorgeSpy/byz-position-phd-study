## The main research path

**WP0 → M1 → A2 and M2a → historical M2b (when feasible).** Independent musicological validation **M3a** runs across this programme. **M4-min** provides the external performance criterion needed for confirmatory historical M2b, but is not a prerequisite for the rest of the core.

## What is guaranteed?

The minimum core comprises **annotated New Method works, WP0, M1, A2, M2a, and M3a**. The historical programme commits to **documenting and auditing co-exegesis relationships** and exploring their overlap with recording collections; it does *not* guarantee enough eligible pairs to train EISE or enough independent recordings for confirmatory `Δhist`.

The early inventory assesses feasibility. Statistical calibration for historical M2b follows selection of `R★`, not the initial six-month audit.

## Gates for the historical experiment

- **`G_pair`**: documented eligible historical pairs and feasible, sufficiently comparable B2–EISE matching.
- **`G_perf`**: recordings of the *same eligible passages* by independent chanters, with reliable alignment.
- **`G_precision`**: calibrated Type-I error, coverage, and power after selecting `R★`.

Depending on these gates, an outcome may be **confirmatory**, **estimation-only**, or **not estimable**. An unavailable primary contrast is not reported as a null finding.

## Related but distinct research

[ECSI](../../../docs/research/ecsi/README.md) extends A2 by jointly estimating musicological concepts; it is **not** the A2 study or EISE. The [Mthesis operator](../current/m-thesis.md), [ATL](../current/atl.md), and [ATL-INV](../current/inverse-problem.md) address probabilistic realisation, attested transformation learning, and older-notation recovery respectively. They are separate extensions, not mandatory parts of the minimum core.

**Important older-vs-current notation:** Earlier M2 chapters use B0 for a direct symbolic historical profile and include a further `Δlearn` contrast. In the revised short study B0 is the symbolic semi-CRF baseline, and **`Δhist` is the sole primary historical M2b contrast**. Consult the source chapters without conflating these versions.
