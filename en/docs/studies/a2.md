## Research question

**A2 is part of the required core (RQ2).** It isolates two questions under the same representation `R★` and known formula boundaries:

1. **Content:** does a valid, independently justified *family specification* improve family recognition relative to structurally matched, controlled perturbations?
2. **Method of use:** does the specified structured logical combination add information beyond predetermined additive summaries of the same diagnostic roles?

A knowledge-free M1 base score stays fixed during the primary comparison. This prevents uncontrolled encoder retraining from obscuring the knowledge intervention.

## Conditions and baselines

| Knowledge use | Valid specification (`V`) | Perturbed specification (`P`) |
|---|---|---|
| **F — additive** | `FV` | `FP` |
| **D — structured logic** | `DV` | `DP` |

There are **four factorial conditions** and **two additional comparators**: `B_K` uses the same extracted concepts without a rule specification; `B★` is a stronger competitive representation-learning baseline. These are not six interchangeable factorial cells.

The F and D conditions access the same extracted concepts, diagnostic roles and alternatives. D adds the defined full-rule violation term; unknown truth is not penalised as a known violation.

The primary protocol envisages ten eligible, structurally controlled perturbations and five matched training seeds. Perturbations are validated for satisfiability and non-equivalence **without choosing them for their effects on test labels**.

## Primary estimands

Let `S(A,K)` denote mean family Average Precision (`AP_fam`), and `S̄(A,P)` the mean over the eligible perturbations:

```text
Δknow    = 1/2 · {[S(D,V) − S̄(D,P)] + [S(F,V) − S̄(F,P)]}
Δlogic,V = S(D,V) − S(F,V)
```

`Δknow` estimates the value of valid knowledge content across both specified uses. `Δlogic,V` estimates the added value of **this particular structured logical combination**, not every possible logic-based model.

Two primary one-sided tests use Holm correction at family-wise `α=0.025`. A subsequent confirmatory family comparing `DV` to `B_K` and `B★` is opened only after both primary hypotheses succeed. Data dependence, baseline comparisons, and the inferential behaviour of the chosen AP estimator must be validated; otherwise the A2 study reports estimation-only results.

## Distinction from ECSI

A2 compares the *validity of specified knowledge* and its *additive versus structured use*. [ECSI](../../../docs/research/ecsi/README.md) is a distinct conditional extension that jointly estimates the diagnostic concepts probabilistically and compiles their compatibility with family specifications. It does not replace A2's primary contrasts.
