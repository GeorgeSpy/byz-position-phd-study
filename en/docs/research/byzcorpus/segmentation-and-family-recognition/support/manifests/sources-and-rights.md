## Provenance of experimental runs

The results derive from three internal execution archives, recorded with neutral identifiers and checksums in [`archives.json`](../../../../../../../docs/research/byzcorpus/segmentation-and-family-recognition/support/manifests/archives.json). Each row in the [experiment register](../../../../../../../docs/research/byzcorpus/segmentation-and-family-recognition/support/manifests/experiments.csv) specifies its source archive, result-file path and SHA-256 checksum.

The experiments are accompanied by condition specifications, parameters, result records and checksums. Metrics were recomputed from the available result files. The register distinguishes executed experiments, reproducible numerical checks, withdrawn batches and planned studies. External sources and dependencies are documented separately.

## Availability of supporting materials

The present release provides experiment manifests and aggregate statistics. Trained model weights, the primary Helsen melodies and analyses, and intermediate alignment files are excluded from distribution. Primary result records and execution scripts are scheduled for the next reproducibility release, following dependency and rights review.
