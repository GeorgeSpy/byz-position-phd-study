The first priority is to assemble a **musicologically valid registry**: type definitions, specific occurrences, assessability of diagnostic observations, and independent expert judgements. The next stages are reproducible signature compilation, comparable family scoring, and the matched 2×2 experiment with preservation of realisation information.

**Proposed MIR paper:** *Context-Compiled Signatures for Historical Musical Formula Families: Identity, Assessability, and Realization Preservation*.

The intended contributions are (1) critically examined musicological evidence and a new set of explicit diagnostics; (2) an exact, auditable signature-based mechanism; and (3) empirical isolation of the added value of structured knowledge on genuine difficult cases. These outcomes are **planned research deliverables**, not existing empirical findings.

**Potential ML paper:** an algorithmic/theoretical identifiability certificate for musically meaningful concepts under multiple contexts, overlapping logical constraints, partial assessability, and dependent judgements. A contribution on this track would have to establish a substantial advance beyond existing results on reasoning shortcuts and indirect supervision. Elementary rank criteria are starting points, **not a new general theorem**.

**Implementation sequence:** P0′ → P1′ C (and independently L if verified) → P2′ development experiments → preregistration of confirmatory testing → MIR manuscript → ML extension only if additional theoretical and empirical guarantees are obtained.
